About the Game

Cooperative play that builds real skills

Playing cooperative games is a great way to teach kids the importance of teamwork. Working as a team is an important yet difficult skill that parents want to teach their kids. It's an essential social skill that helps kids master other necessary skills like problem solving, strategy building, and planning ahead — required for their development in those early years of life.

Fastrek takes you on an adventurous trek where you have to fight shape monsters, complete tasks to collect various camping essentials, and reach your campsite before it gets dark. Kids make a winning strategy together as a team and complete the tasks by mutual cooperation — everyone wins together.

Teamwork
Kids coordinate and cooperate toward a shared goal — everyone wins or no one does.
Strategy
Plan each move together, adapt to challenges, and develop critical thinking in a fun setting.
Adventure
A trek-and-camp theme kids love — fight shape monsters and collect gear along the way.

Our Story

Born from a family adventure

Hello — I'm Swati. Together with my 2 kids, we are the game designers of Fastrek. It all started during Covid in 2021 when my spouse told my kids that "we have bought enough games and they need to be creative and build their own pretend-play game." We never could have imagined the far-reaching consequence of this benign statement.

From a mock-game built on a plain sheet of paper to the professionally designed game was quite a journey — 2 years of working with teams across boundaries. The game was designed by our kids and I in Seattle, the graphics were designed in Manchester, UK, and the game was finally manufactured in China.

Fastrek is a game that begins with a trek and ends at a campsite — something we always do in real life. That is the essence of the game and also how our kids picked up the idea.
