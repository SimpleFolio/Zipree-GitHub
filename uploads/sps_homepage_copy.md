## SECTION: LEVERAGE TRANSPARENCY

### Returns earned honestly, not borrowed.

Before subscribing to any trading strategy, there's one number worth checking that most subscribers overlook: **leverage**.

Leverage amplifies both gains and losses. A strategy running high margin can show impressive returns in good markets — but a single bad week can wipe out months of gains, or worse. Understanding how a strategy generates its returns is just as important as the returns themselves.

**Our leverage discipline:**

| Strategy | Margin Used | Leverage Source |
|---|---|---|
| Simply Long | **Under 1x** | No margin — cash-basis stock positions |
| Simply Strategic | **Under 1x** | No margin — cash-basis stock positions |
| Simply Leveraged | **Up to 3x** | Built into the ETFs (TQQQ, SOXL, etc.) — *no account margin* |

We don't borrow to amplify returns. The leverage in Simply Leveraged comes from the ETFs themselves, which is fully disclosed and capped by the funds. Your account is never exposed to a margin call from our trades.

**What this means for you:**
- Drawdowns stay recoverable, not catastrophic.
- A bad month is a bad month — not an account wipeout.
- You can stay invested through volatility instead of watching your account hourly.

*A simple question worth asking any signal provider before you subscribe: how much margin does the strategy use?*
